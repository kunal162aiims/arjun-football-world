🎓 Football Academy

Understand the beautiful game.

🚩 Offside

A player is generally offside if, when a teammate plays the ball, they are nearer the opponents' goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent, unless an exception applies.

🎥 VAR

Video Assistant Referee supports officials with reviews of certain important match decisions.

🎩 Hat-trick

Three goals by the same player in one match.

🟥 Red Card

A sent-off player cannot continue the match.

🟨 Yellow Card

A caution shown to a player for an offence.

🥅 Penalty

A penalty kick is awarded for certain fouls inside the defending team's penalty area.

👟 Positions

Goalkeepers protect the goal; defenders protect space; midfielders connect play; forwards focus heavily on attacking and scoring.

🔄 Substitution

A substitute can enter the match according to the competition's rules and the referee's procedure.
