🎓 Football Academy

Understand the beautiful game.

🚩 Offside

A player is generally offside if, when a teammate plays the ball, they are nearer the opponents' goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent, unless an exception applies.

🎥 VAR

Video Assistant Referee supports officials with reviews of certain important match decisions.

🎩 Hat-trick

Three goals by the same player in one match.

🟥 Red Card

A sent-off player cannot continue the match.

🟨 Yellow Card

A caution shown to a player for an offence.

🥅 Penalty

A penalty kick is awarded for certain fouls inside the defending team's penalty area.

👟 Positions

Goalkeepers protect the goal; defenders protect space; midfielders connect play; forwards focus heavily on attacking and scoring.

🔄 Substitution

A substitute can enter the match according to the competition's rules and the referee's procedure.

🧠 Deep-Dive Football

For the die-hard fan: tactics, analytics, terminology, competition knowledge and historical trivia.

📊

xG — Expected Goals

A model estimating the probability that a shot becomes a goal from characteristics of the chance. It is an estimate, not a prediction of an individual shot.

📈

PPDA

Passes per defensive action is commonly used as a pressing-intensity indicator. Lower numbers generally mean fewer opponent passes are allowed before a defensive action.

🧠

Third-Man Run

A player becomes the next receiver after a teammate uses a layoff or pass to release them, often bypassing an opponent.

🔺

Rest Defence

The structure a team keeps behind the ball while attacking so it can control counterattacks after possession is lost.

🧩

Overload to Isolate

A team overloads one zone to attract defenders, then attacks an isolated player or space elsewhere.

↔️

Switch of Play

Quickly transferring the ball from one side of the pitch to the other to attack space created by defensive movement.

🎯

Half-Space

The channel between the central lane and the wing, offering strong angles for passing, crossing and shots.

🧱

Low Block

A compact defensive setup close to the team's own goal, prioritising protection of central space.

⚡

Gegenpressing

Immediate pressure after losing possession, aiming to recover the ball before the opponent can organise.

👟

Regista

An Italian football term for a deep-lying playmaker who directs possession and progression from a deeper zone.

🔄

Double Pivot

Two midfielders share the base of the midfield, helping build up, protect transitions and circulate the ball.

🎩

Mezzala

A shuttling central midfielder who often attacks the half-space and contributes beyond the central midfield line.
