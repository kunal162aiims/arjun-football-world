Understand the game one concept at a time.

🚩 OffsideA player is generally offside if, when a teammate plays the ball, they are nearer the opponents' goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent, unless an exception applies.
🎥 VARVideo Assistant Referee supports officials in reviewing certain important match decisions.
🎩 Hat-trickThree goals scored by the same player in one match.
🟥 Red CardThe player is sent off and cannot continue the match.
🟨 Yellow CardA caution shown to a player for an offence.
🥅 PenaltyA penalty kick is awarded for certain fouls inside the defending team's penalty area.
👟 PositionsGoalkeeper protects the goal; defenders protect space; midfielders link play; forwards focus heavily on attacking and scoring.
